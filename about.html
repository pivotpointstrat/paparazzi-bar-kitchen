Our Story

A riverside hideaway with a camera-flash soul

Opened in 2024 on Riverside, Paparazzi Bar & Kitchen brings together international comfort food, a serious drinks list, and the kind of dim, relaxed atmosphere where a good evening stretches late into the night.

Food

From slow-cooked ribs and house lasagna to Swedish meatballs and a full charcuterie board — a menu that swings from pub classics to Scandinavian and Khmer touches, all made from scratch.

Drinks

Twenty-four craft cocktails at $5, Cambodian draught, imported bottles, and a rotating wine list. Happy hour specials run all week.

Atmosphere

Dim, warm lighting, friendly English-speaking staff, and a private upper level for groups — plus a cigar lounge upstairs for a slower kind of evening.

The Cigar Lounge

Upstairs, unwind

Our cozy cigar lounge carries a selection of Dominican and Caribbean cigars — a quiet corner to pair a smoke with a drink and watch the river go by.
